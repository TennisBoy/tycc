# Complete the Work

## When to Use

- The user says `Complete the work`, `complete the work`, or `wrap up the session`.

## What This Does

Runs the end-of-session ritual: distill durable memory, write a session summary, update playbooks, commit, and push.

## Procedure

Read `team-lead/playbooks/complete-the-work.md` and follow every step in order.

Do not skip steps. Do not summarize instead of executing. The playbook is the source of truth for this workflow.
